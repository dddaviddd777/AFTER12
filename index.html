Bio

AFTER 12 is what remains when the lights go out and only the rhythm is left.

It began in 2018 with a laptop, FL Studio and almost no idea of the rules. The result was Delusions, an experimental debut driven by pure curiosity and the thrill of discovering new sounds.

Years later, AFTER 12 returned with state-of-the-art tools and renewed creative direction. Blending deep house, hypnotic grooves, captivating vocals and thought-provoking lyrics, the project explores the space between darkness and light, introspection and euphoria.
